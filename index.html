melostem

Live karaoke for anything playing on your computer

Press Share audio and pick a YouTube tab, or your entire screen with "Share system audio" to use Spotify, VLC or any other player.

For music apps outside the browser, like Spotify, VLC or Apple Music: install a free virtual audio cable (BlackHole on a Mac, VB-Cable on Windows) and set the music app's sound output, or your computer's, to it. Then pick the cable below, and your real speakers or headphones to listen on. Only melostem's karaoke version reaches your ears.

Getting the AI ready…

Runs Meta's open HT-Demucs model on your graphics card. It downloads once (about 126 MB) and is then cached in your browser. Needs Chrome or Edge with WebGPU. Audio never leaves your computer.

Instruments

Keys: V karaoke, 1–4 switch an instrument on or off.

AI model: HT-Demucs by Meta AI (MIT), web build by monteslu.
